Element 01 · Hero Cover

Animated Hero Stats

Three stat cards stagger-animate in from the right on page load using Framer Motion. Each card carries an icon, a bold metric, and a label.

How it works

Each card uses motion.div with initial={{ opacity:0, x:20 }} and a staggered delay: 0.4 + i * 0.1. The whole column animates in from the right while the headline fades up from below — creating a split-entry feel.
